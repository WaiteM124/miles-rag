# Fixed Asset Registration

Fixed assets are registered from the Assets workspace. An asset requires a unique asset code, a purchase date, a cost, and a depreciation profile.

Depreciation profiles determine how the asset's value is written down over time. The standard profiles are straight line over 3, 5, or 10 years, and reducing balance at 25 percent.

To register an asset:
1. Open Assets and select New Asset.
2. Enter the asset code, description, purchase date, and cost.
3. Select the depreciation profile. The system calculates the first depreciation charge from the purchase date.
4. Select Register. The asset appears in the fixed asset register and depreciation begins on the next monthly run.

Disposals are recorded from the asset record. The disposal proceeds and date determine any gain or loss on disposal, which posts automatically to the profit and loss account.
